Claim Orchestrator

An AI agent checks each insurance claim against its policy and coverage rules, then decides. Unsure cases go to a person.

Let an AI agent investigate insurance claims, and see exactly why it decided.

Pick a pending claim and run an investigation. A local LLM looks up the policy, checks the coverage rules, and returns a verdict with its reasoning, a confidence score and the rules it relied on.
